Rules & Regulations

General Rules

  • Only school-registered teams are allowed.
  • Each school must send at least one chaperone with its teams.
  • Teams must arrive at the venue well in time.
  • Bring your own laptops, internet devices / mobile hotspots — with Arduino, Photoshop, Unity or other required software pre-installed.
  • Students must wear name tags with their branch name.
  • No external help (from teachers etc.) during the competition.
  • Participant names must be sent by {{ info.deadline }}.
  • Multiple teams may participate from one school.

Code of Conduct

  • Attire: formal dress, Eastern or Western, is mandatory.
  • Conduct: misconduct may lead to removal from the premises; the Executive Council's decisions are final.
  • Photography: no photos/videos of staff or participants without consent — official coverage is by the event media team only.
  • Zero tolerance for harassment, bullying, or abusive remarks — violations lead to expulsion of the entire delegation.
  • Liability waiver: every participant and faculty advisor must submit a signed waiver at the registration desk before the opening ceremony.
