Code of Conduct

Sportsmanship

  • Play fairly and follow all rules and regulations.
  • All games are open to both male and female participants.
  • Respect referees and officials — accept their decisions without argument.
  • Treat teammates, opponents, organizers, and officials with respect.
  • Demonstrate good sportsmanship, teamwork, and discipline throughout.

Discipline & Requirements

  • No abusive language, trash-talking, bullying, or physical aggression.
  • Participants uphold the values and reputation of their schools at all times.
  • Each player may participate in a maximum of 2 games.
  • Every participant must submit a signed Waiver of Liability at the registration desk before playing.
  • Players are responsible for the safekeeping of their personal belongings.
